SafeAR — printable Hiro marker

Print this page at 100% scale (not "fit to page") on A4. The black border must stay crisp and the sheet must lie flat — a curled or glossy marker is the most common reason tracking fails.

  1. Print on plain matte white paper. Avoid glossy photo paper: it reflects and blinds the camera.
  2. Tape it to stiff card so it cannot curl.
  3. Light it evenly. Direct overhead spotlights create glare across the black border.
  4. Keep the whole square in frame, roughly 30–60 cm from the phone.

If tracking still fails on demo day, switch the app to "No marker" mode on the module screen — the same scenario runs without the camera.
